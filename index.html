1. Server settings

  • Turn on Community (needed for announcement channels and rules screening).
  • Verification level: Medium or High. Explicit media filter: all members.
  • Default notifications: only @mentions.
  • Safety Setup: require 2FA for moderator actions (your account needs 2FA on first).
  • Don't make "Unverified" the thing that locks channels. Let @everyone see only Information and Verification, and hide everything else from @everyone and show it to Verified Player. The Unverified role is then just a label the bot adds on join, and nothing depends on it until the bot exists.

Who can vouch in Season 0: Founder, Co-Creator, Administrator, Moderator, Gatekeeper A4.

On "do we need ban/mute roles?": mutes and kicks use Discord's timeout and kick. Permanent bans use Discord bans. The one extra role I'd add is Suspended, because Discord has no built-in timer for temp bans, and a role lets the bot time it and still let the person appeal.

6. Main flows

FlowSteps
Verification (Season 0)Join → Unverified → [ VERIFY ACCOUNT ] → private thread → screenshot of all characters + account name, and name of the vouching member → Gatekeeper/Mod approves in #verification-queue → Verified + Season 0 Player
Ranked matchTeam registered → [ JOIN LOBBY ] → lobby thread (Lobby Leader, Match ID e.g. DCL-MATCH-024) → vote or auto-start (6 teams, or max wait) → bot creates team voice channels → match played via custom matchmaking → each player submits placement, kills and screenshot → staff verify → rating updated → posted in #match-results
No reportMaximum rating penalty applied to that player only. A staff override (for example a proven crash or disconnect with a clip) is optional and always logged in #rating-adjustments
Report a player[ REPORT PLAYER ] → #mod-reports → Review → Decision. A report never results in automatic punishment
Arena[ JOIN ARENA ] → opponents matched → [ REQUEST MIDDLEMAN ] to hold stakes → BO3 → result logged in #arena-results and #middleman-log

7. What the bot will need to do

Preview for the next phase.

  • Buttons/forms: Verify Account, Register Team, Join Lobby, Start Match Vote, Submit Results, Join Arena, Request Middleman, Report Player, Open Ticket, leaderboard sort (Team/Individual), staff action buttons.
  • Commands: /elo, /profile, /leaderboard, staff-only /rating-adjust, /suspend, /season.
  • Database: players, teams (rosters, active/retired), seasons, matches, per-player results, ratings (team and individual, per season), reports/cases, penalties, Arena matches.
  • Rating model: rating is driven by placement and team kills only, never personal kills (healers don't kill). Team rating belongs to a registered roster. Individual rating belongs to the player, gaining or losing the same change as whatever team they played in, and carries across every roster they play with. Personal kills are still recorded and shown on profiles as stats.
  • Config file: rating formula, lobby max wait, vote threshold, penalty size, so none of it is hard-coded.

8. Setup order

  1. Create the roles in the order in section 2.
  2. Turn on Community and the section 1 settings.
  3. Create the 13 categories and set permissions on each category using the section 3 table.
  4. Create the channels inside them (sync permissions to the category).
  5. Post placeholder text in the bot channels ("Coming soon") so they aren't empty.
  6. Test with a spare account: first as Unverified, then as Verified.
  7. Write the text for #welcome, #rules, #how-dcl-works, #faq.
  8. Build the bot (next phase).

9. Assumptions to confirm

TagAssumption
A1Only the Founder has Discord's Administrator flag. Co-Creator and Administrator get the individual permissions they need.
A2Moderators can warn, timeout, suspend and apply rating penalties. Permanent bans need Admin+ sign-off.
A3Gatekeeper is a verify-only role (the "just lets people in" role you described).
A4Vouchers: Founder, Co-Creator, Administrator, Moderator, Gatekeeper.
A5Season 0 Player is a badge; Verified Player is what unlocks channels. In Season 1 the badge stays as a founders' mark.
A6Temp suspensions use the Suspended role (bot-timed). Permanent bans use a normal Discord ban.
A7Each team names one Clipper who records the match and keeps the VOD for 7 days; it's only uploaded for disputes.
A8Lobby max wait time: 30 minutes, then it starts with the available eligible teams (configurable).
A9LFT/LFP posts use a bot form and auto-expire after 24 hours.
A10Class roles are optional and cosmetic. Check the list against the current in-game classes.
A11Permanently banned players can't see #appeals, so you need an outside route (a form or email) for them.
A12Only Admin+ can hand out the Middleman role, and you keep the number small, since middlemen hold other people's stakes.
